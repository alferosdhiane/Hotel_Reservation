Booking Policies

  1. No Walk-In Reservations
    All bookings must be made online or via phone at least one day prior to the intended check-in date.
  2. Advance Payment Policy
    Reservations are confirmed only after the required full or partial payment has been received.
  3. Cancellation and Refund Policy
    Cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the scheduled check-in date to avoid cancellation charges.
  4. Maximum Occupancy Rule
    Room occupancy must not exceed the stated room capacity. Additional guests require prior approval and may incur extra charges.
  5. Check-In and Check-Out Policy
    Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and additional fees.
