Infinity Swimming Pool

Relax and unwind at our beautifully designed infinity swimming pool, where comfort and luxury come together. Surrounded by elegant lounge areas and scenic views, our pool offers the perfect setting for a refreshing swim, family fun, or a peaceful afternoon under the sun.

Pool Features

  • Outdoor infinity swimming pool
  • Separate children's pool area
  • Comfortable poolside lounge chairs
  • Poolside food and beverage service
  • Clean changing rooms and shower facilities
  • Night lighting for evening swimming
  • Trained staff available during operating hours

Operating Hours

Open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Pool Policies

  • Proper swimwear is required at all times.
  • Children under 12 years old must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Running, diving, and rough play are prohibited.
  • Outside food and beverages are not allowed in the pool area.
  • Please shower before entering the pool.
  • Glass containers are strictly prohibited.
  • Guests must follow all instructions given by hotel staff and lifeguards.
  • The hotel is not responsible for lost or unattended personal belongings.
  • For safety reasons, guests who appear intoxicated may be denied pool access.
  • Pool towels are provided exclusively for registered hotel guests.

Perfect for Everyone

Whether you're looking to exercise, spend quality time with your family, or simply relax poolside with a refreshing drink, our swimming pool offers a safe, enjoyable, and luxurious experience for guests of all ages.
